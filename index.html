Pen Translate

Hover your S Pen over any text. Read it in your language. No taps.

Pen Translate is an Android app for stylus tablets and phones. Hold the pen a few millimetres above a word and a translation appears next to it. Nothing is tapped, nothing is selected, nothing is copied.

How it works

  • The pen's hover position comes from Android's accessibility motion-event stream.
  • The word under the pen is read from the on-screen accessibility node, or recognised from a screenshot when the app underneath exposes no text.
  • Translation runs on-device with Google ML Kit. Language models are downloaded once and used offline afterwards.
