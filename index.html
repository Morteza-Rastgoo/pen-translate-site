Point at any text on your screen. Read it in your language.

Point Translate is an Android app. Drag a small sight over any word, sentence, or paragraph — in any app — and let go. The translation appears in a floating bubble, right where you are looking. No tapping into a menu, no copying, no switching apps, no screenshots. With an S Pen you can just hover and hold still.

Two ways to point

With a finger

A small sight sits on top of the screen. Drag it over the text you want and let go. Works on any phone or tablet — no stylus needed.

With a stylus

Hover the tip just above the text and hold still for a moment. Nothing to tap at all. Supported on devices with pen hover, such as Samsung Galaxy models with an S Pen.

From anywhere

Bring the sight up from a Quick Settings tile or the Android accessibility shortcut, then point.

Works everywhere you can see text

Browsers, chat apps, PDF readers, e-books, email, social feeds, games, menus, settings screens. If you can see the text, you can translate it. Point Translate first asks the app for the text directly (fast and exact); when an app doesn't expose its text, it falls back to on-device character recognition so you still get a result.

Private by design

Translation runs entirely on your device using on-device machine learning. Nothing you point at is uploaded, stored, or shared. The app collects no personal data at all — no account, no analytics, no advertising. Language packs download once and then work completely offline, with no connection.

60+ languages, both directions

Pick any source and target language, or let the app detect the source automatically. Translate into the language you read, from the language on the screen — in either direction.

Free and Pro

The free version gives you 10 translations every day, with the full feature set. Pro removes the daily limit with a monthly plan, a yearly plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase.
