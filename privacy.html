Privacy Policy

Point Translate · com.hellodelorus.pentranslate · last updated 25 November 2026

Point Translate does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything the app reads from your screen is processed on your device, in memory, and discarded. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no advertising.

What the app reads, and why

Point Translate runs as an Android AccessibilityService. That is the only API on Android that reports a pointing position (stylus hover, or a finger-dragged sight) across other apps, which is what makes a zero-tap translation possible. The service is used for exactly two things:

  • A pointing position — where the S Pen is hovering, or where you drag the on-screen sight, and when it has paused long enough to mean "translate this".
  • The text under the pointer — read from the accessibility node tree, or, when the app underneath publishes no text node, recognised from a single screenshot of the area around the pointer.

The service does not read, log, or forward anything else. It does not record keystrokes, passwords, form fields you are typing into, or screen content away from the pointer. Screens marked secure by their own app (banking apps, password managers) cannot be captured at all — Android blocks it, and the app aborts when it detects such a capture.

Where translation happens

Translation uses Google ML Kit's on-device Translation API. The text never leaves your device. The only network activity the app causes is the one-time download of a language model from Google's servers, performed by ML Kit itself; after that the feature works with no connection. Those downloads are governed by Google's privacy policy.

Payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Point Translate never sees your card, address, or Google account. It receives only a yes/no answer about whether the current Play account owns a subscription or the lifetime unlock.

Children

Point Translate is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control

  • The accessibility service does nothing until you enable it and accept the in-app consent screen. Declining turns the feature off.
  • You can revoke it at any time in Android Settings, Accessibility, Point Translate.
  • Uninstalling removes every stored preference and counter.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and a material change will be surfaced in the app before it takes effect.
